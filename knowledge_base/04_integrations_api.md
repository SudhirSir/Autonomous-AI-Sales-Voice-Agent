# Native Integrations & Ecosystem API

## CRM & Revenue Stack
- **Salesforce**: Native managed package supporting bidirectional Leads, Contacts, Opportunities, Tasks, and custom BANT objects in under 15 minutes setup.
- **HubSpot**: OAuth 2.0 one-click integration syncing Deals, Engagements, Call Summaries, and Lifecycle Stages in real time.
- **Pipedrive, Zoho CRM & Microsoft Dynamics 365**: Supported via pre-built connectors and signed webhook events.

## Calendar, Telephony & Identity
- **Calendar & Scheduling**: Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook / Office 365, and Calendly conflict-free instant booking.
- **Voice & Telephony**: SIP trunking, Twilio, RingCentral, Aircall, and browser WebRTC.
- **Identity & Data**: Okta, Azure AD (Entra ID) SAML 2.0 SSO, Snowflake & BigQuery nightly warehouse sync, plus REST & WebSocket APIs.

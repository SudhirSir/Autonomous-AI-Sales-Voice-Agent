# Competitive Differentiation & Battlecards

## Fortivex vs. Gong / Chorus
- Gong focuses on passive post-call recording and coaching, whereas Fortivex executes **autonomous real-time voice engagement**, live BANT qualification, and instant calendar booking during the conversation.
- Fortivex deploys in 5 days at roughly 40% lower total cost of ownership without separate add-on fees for forecasting or CRM write-back.

## Fortivex vs. Legacy Rule-Based Chatbots (Drift / Intercom)
- Legacy bots rely on rigid decision trees that frustrate enterprise buyers and only work in text chat.
- Fortivex uses hybrid RAG grounding and multilingual real-time voice (English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi) with contextual memory across calls.

## Fortivex vs. Building In-House
- Building in-house requires stitching STT, TTS, RAG, guardrails, CRM webhooks, and compliance audits—typically 6–9 months of engineering time (\$250k+). Fortivex delivers production-ready voice + CRM + SOC2 out of the box in 5 days.

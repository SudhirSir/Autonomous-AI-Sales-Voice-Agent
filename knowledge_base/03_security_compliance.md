# Security, Privacy & Regulatory Compliance

## Certifications & Attestations
- **SOC 2 Type II**: Independently audited annually with zero exceptions across security, availability, and confidentiality trust criteria.
- **ISO 27001 & GDPR**: Full EU data residency (Frankfurt & Dublin regions), automated DSAR deletion workflows, and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
- **HIPAA & PCI-DSS Ready**: Business Associate Agreement (BAA) available on Enterprise tier; automated PCI/PII redaction before transcript storage.

## Encryption & Zero-Training Guarantee
- All audio, transcripts, and CRM payloads are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest via AES-256 with customer-managed KMS keys (BYOK).
- **Zero Data Training Policy**: Customer conversations and knowledge base documents are NEVER used to train foundation models.

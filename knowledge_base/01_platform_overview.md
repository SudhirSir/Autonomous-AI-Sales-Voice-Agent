# Fortivex Enterprise AI Revenue & Security Platform

## Overview
Fortivex Cloud is an autonomous AI revenue operations and enterprise security platform designed for B2B sales, customer success, and RevOps teams. It unifies real-time conversational AI, automated CRM hygiene, SOC2-compliant data governance, and predictive pipeline intelligence in a single deployment.

## Key Capabilities
- **Autonomous Revenue Intelligence**: Captures 100% of buyer calls, emails, and meetings with zero manual CRM data entry.
- **Enterprise Guardrails & Compliance**: Built-in PII redaction, role-based access control (RBAC), and region-pinned data residency.
- **Sub-300ms Real-Time Voice & Workflow Engine**: Low-latency streaming architecture capable of handling 10,000+ concurrent sessions with 99.99% uptime SLA.
- **Deployment Timeline**: Standard cloud onboarding takes 5 business days; VPC / private cloud deployment takes 14 business days with a dedicated Solutions Architect.
